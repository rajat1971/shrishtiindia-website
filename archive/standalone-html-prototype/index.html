Founder & CEO

Rajat Bagaria

“[Your line — one sentence on how you think about travel.]”

Rajat started in travel in 1996–97 and built Shrishti into an IATA-accredited agency. From 2007 to 2020 he served the Travel Agents Federation of India — rising from office bearer to Chairman of the Mumbai Chapter, national committee member and Joint Secretary — and spent over a decade on the teams that ran TAFI's international conventions, co-chairing Jerusalem and Abu Dhabi.

He is also a co-founder of MTF Events, an independent company set up with two fellow agency owners, which organises the Melange travel trade exhibition.

Beyond Shrishti · an independent venture

MTF Events — reimagining how the travel trade meets.

MTF Events is an independent company founded by three Mumbai agency owners, Rajat among them. It began in 2023 as a friendly gathering to launch MTF Premier League Cricket for the travel trade.

Seeing trade exhibitions grow dull and dated, the group launched Melange in 2024. Its success made it an annual fixture — and Melange Select now takes the format to regional markets, alongside trade-focused formats such as Spark.
